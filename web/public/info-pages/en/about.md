# About SearGo

SearGo is a privacy-focused metasearch engine that aggregates results from multiple search engines without tracking you.

We believe search should be transparent, fast, and respectful of your personal data. SearGo does not build profiles, store queries, or share information with third-party advertisers.

Built with Go and React, SearGo is inspired by the SearXNG project and aims to provide a lightweight, self-hostable search experience.
